Community Venture Proposal

Everyday care, packed into one affordable kit.

CareBloom is a small community venture that focuses on providing affordable personal care and hygiene products. Basic hygiene products are needed every day, but buying them one by one isn't always convenient or easy to afford — so CareBloom brings them together into one simple, budget-friendly kit.

See the Kit View the Budget
“Care Today, Bloom Tomorrow.”
The Problem

Basic hygiene items are needed daily, but hard to keep up with.

One problem we noticed in the community is that some students and families have limited budgets for personal care and hygiene products.

💸

Limited budgets

Students and families often have to choose which hygiene items to buy this week, since a full budget rarely covers everything at once.

🛒

Buying one by one is inconvenient

Purchasing soap, toothpaste, and other essentials separately takes more time, more trips, and makes it easy to forget an item.

Proposed Solution

One simple kit, already put together.

CareBloom will offer affordable hygiene and self-care kits. The kits will contain basic items that people can use in their daily lives, so buyers don't need to shop around for each product separately.

This makes basic care simpler, faster, and easier to fit into a tight budget — everything needed is already bundled into one package.

Included in Every Kit

A Small Health Reminder Card

Alongside the hygiene items, each kit comes with a simple reminder card about proper hygiene and taking care of oneself — a small nudge toward better daily habits.

Product

The Care Bloom Basic Care Kit.

Our main product is the Care Bloom Basic Care Kit — a bundle of everyday essentials in one package.

Basic Care Kit
One bundle, everyday essentials
  • 🧼 Soap
  • 🪥 Toothbrush
  • 🦷 Toothpaste
  • 🧻 Tissue
  • 🧴 Alcohol or hand sanitizer
  • 💇 Small comb
  • 📋 Health reminder card

The contents can be changed depending on the customer's needs and budget — items can be added or removed to build a kit that fits.

Marketing Strategy

Reaching people the way word already travels.

Facebook & Messenger

Posting updates, kit photos, and availability through Facebook and Messenger where our community already spends time.

Word of mouth

Sharing CareBloom through school classmates, friends, and community members who can vouch for it firsthand.

Posters & packaging

Simple posters and attractive but affordable packaging to help CareBloom stand out without adding to the cost.

We can also offer different sizes of kits so customers can choose based on their budget.
Expected Outcome

More than a business — a small habit-building project too.

Through CareBloom, we hope to provide useful and affordable hygiene products to the community. We also expect to improve awareness about proper personal hygiene while learning how to manage a small business as students.
